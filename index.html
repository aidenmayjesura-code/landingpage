Make Your Community Better.

CG ReportHub is a platform for residents of Barangay Catalunan Grande to report and track community concerns such as garbage disposal, damaged roads, broken streetlights, and drainage problems.

Report a Problem Learn More

About CG ReportHub

Our mission is to empower the community to actively participate in maintaining and improving our barangay. By reporting issues, you help create a cleaner, safer, and more pleasant environment for everyone.

Common Community Problems

Improper Garbage Disposal

Report instances of incorrect waste disposal that affect cleanliness and health.

Damaged Roads

Notify us about potholes, cracks, or other road damages that need repair.

Broken Streetlights

Report non-functional streetlights to improve safety at night.

Drainage Problems

Alert us about clogged drains or flooding issues during rainy seasons.

How It Works

1

Report

Submit a report about a community issue through our simple form.

2

Review

Our team reviews the report and assigns it to the appropriate department.

3

Resolve

The issue is addressed, and you can track its progress.

About the Proposed System

CG ReportHub aims to establish a streamlined reporting system that connects residents with local authorities. By leveraging technology, we hope to reduce response times and increase transparency in issue resolution.

This platform is designed to be user-friendly, accessible, and responsive to the needs of Barangay Catalunan Grande.
